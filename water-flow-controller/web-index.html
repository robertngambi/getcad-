About AquaCtrl

AquaCtrl is a compact, microcontroller-based water management device that automatically controls up to two water pumps based on real-time tank levels. It combines a traditional float switch with a non-contact ultrasonic sensor, giving you the flexibility to monitor two different tanks simultaneously.

How It Works

1️⃣

Sensor Input

The float switch detects high/low water via a mechanical contact. The ultrasonic sensor measures distance to the water surface in centimetres.

2️⃣

Smart Decision

The microcontroller converts readings into a water percentage (based on your chosen tank size) and decides whether to turn the pump ON or OFF.

3️⃣

Relay Output

The relay switches the pump's mains supply safely, protecting the low-voltage electronics from high-current loads.

4️⃣

Wireless Monitor

The onboard Bluetooth module streams status to your phone in real time and receives commands from the app.

Hardware Design

🧠

Microcontroller

ATmega16U4 running at 8 MHz — plenty of I/O, ADC channels for analog sensors, and a built-in USB interface for firmware updates.

📡

Bluetooth Module

KT6368A BLE 5.0 module on UART at 115200 baud. Bridges the controller to the AquaCtrl mobile app over a secure paired link.

🌊

Inputs

Float switch (NO + NC contacts) and HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor. Analog filtering with pull-downs and capacitors removes noise.

🔌

Outputs

Two relays rated for mains pump loads, plus a small cooling fan driven by temperature sensor readings.

🌡️

Temperature Safety

A TMP35 sensor monitors the enclosure temperature. If it exceeds 45 °C, the fan kicks in automatically.

⚡

Power

Input: 7–12 V DC via jack or AC adapter. Regulated 5 V rail for the MCU and sensors using an LM1117DT.
