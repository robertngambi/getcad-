About SimHub Connect

SimHub Connect

v2.0.0 · A Division of GetCAD Technologies Ltd

What this app does
SimHub Connect pairs with your SimHub multi-SIM MiFi device over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi and gives you full control of:

• Contacts, calls and SMS across SimHub SIMs and phone SIMs
• Data routing and hotspot access control
• Hub Services — voice calls, video calls, chat and announcements restricted to SimHub-hosted SIMs
• Enterprise network management with mesh, VLANs and billing dashboard
Connectivity
Primary Transport
BLE GATT (low latency, always paired)
Fallback Transport
Wi-Fi HTTP at 192.168.4.1
Auto-Reconnect
Silent re-pairing on app launch
